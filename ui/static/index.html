Give the model a photo and a caption. It works out which part of a product the post is about — even when the caption never says it — and whether the feeling is positive, negative or neutral.

New here? How this works
1. It reads bothThe caption becomes numbers (word by word); the photo becomes numbers (small patches).
2. It links themWords look at the parts of the photo that matter, and the photo looks back at the words.
3. It answersOne guess among 8 topics — battery, screen, camera, price, delivery, packaging, sound, performance — plus the feeling.

“Generated” examples come from a dataset made by a script, so the true answer is known. “Real tweets” are genuine posts (Twitter-2015/2017); for those the model only judges the feeling, because real tweets have no topic labels.

How well does it work?

Models are compared on accuracy, precision, recall and F1. F1 is the single best number to look at: it balances precision and recall, and 100 is perfect.
